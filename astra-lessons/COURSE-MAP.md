# Course map

Each language has 112 lessons in 16 units. Each new unit includes seven lessons and three age versions.

| Unit | Topic | Goal | English pattern | Putonghua pattern |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Food & drink | Order food and drinks | Existing foundation unit | Existing foundation unit |
| 2 | Hello & introductions / 打招呼與自我介紹 | Introduce yourself and greet someone politely. | Use I am for yourself and you are for the other person. Say my name to introduce yourself. | Use 我是 + role and 我叫 + name. Put 吗 at the end to make a yes/no question. |
| 3 | Numbers & age / 數字與年齡 | Count things and say an age or quantity. | Use one book but two books. Ask How many? for a number of things. | Put a measure word between the number and the noun: 三本书. Use 两 before many measure words, as in 两本书. |
| 4 | Family & people / 家人與朋友 | Introduce family members and describe who they are. | Use my, your, his and her before a noun. Say This is for one person and These are for more than one. | Use 的 for possession: 我的家人. With close family, 我的妈妈 and 我妈妈 are both natural. |
| 5 | School & learning / 學校與學習 | Name learning materials and ask for help politely. | Use Can I ...? to ask permission and Could you ...? to ask for help. Add please. | Use 请 + action for a polite request. Use 可以吗 to ask permission: 我可以看这本书吗？ |
| 6 | Rooms & belongings / 家居與物品 | Describe rooms and say where things are. | Use in for inside, on for a surface and under for below: The bag is under the table. | Put the place after 在: 书在桌子上. Add 上、下、里 after the place word. |
| 7 | Time & daily routines / 時間與日常生活 | Describe a day and ask when something happens. | Use the present simple for routines: I eat, she eats. Use at for a time and every for a repeated day. | Time usually comes before the action: 我早上吃饭. 今天 and 每天 can also start the sentence. |
| 8 | Animals & abilities / 動物與本領 | Describe animals and say what they can do. | Use can + the action: A bird can fly. Can does not change with he, she or it. | Use 会 + action for a learned skill, and 能 for ability or possibility. Birds fly: 鸟会飞. |
| 9 | Around town / 社區與方向 | Name places and give simple directions. | Use Where is ...? to ask about a place. Directions use the action directly: Turn left. Go straight. | Use 怎么走 to ask the way. Say 往左转 or 往右转 to tell someone to turn. |
| 10 | Shopping & colours / 購物與顏色 | Describe what you want and ask about price. | Put a colour before the noun: a red shirt. Use this for one nearby item and these for several. | Use 多少钱 to ask the price. Colours often take 的 before a noun: 红色的衣服. |
| 11 | Weather & seasons / 天氣與季節 | Describe the weather and choose suitable activities. | Use It is + weather word: It is sunny. Use because to give a reason and if for a condition. | Weather descriptions often use 很: 今天很冷. Use 下雨 for rain and 因为 for a reason. |
| 12 | Hobbies & invitations / 興趣與邀請 | Talk about interests and invite someone to join you. | After enjoy, use the -ing form: I enjoy swimming. Invite someone with Would you like to ...? | Use 喜欢 + activity for an interest. Use 一起 to suggest doing it together. |
| 13 | Feelings & asking for help / 感受與求助 | Describe how you feel and ask for support clearly. | Use I feel + an adjective: I feel tired. Use need + a noun or need to + a verb. | Use 我觉得 + feeling, or 我很 + adjective. Use 我需要 to explain what support you need. |
| 14 | Transport & journeys / 交通與旅程 | Choose transport, ask for a ticket and explain a journey. | Use by + transport: by bus, by train. Use take the bus when the vehicle is the object of the action. | Use 坐 for travelling by a vehicle: 坐火车. 从 introduces the starting point; 到 introduces the destination. |
| 15 | Yesterday & stories / 昨天與故事 | Tell a short past event in a clear order. | Past events use past forms: go becomes went, play becomes played. Use first, then and finally to order events. | Time words such as 昨天 locate an event in the past. 了 often marks a completed action; it is not a past-tense ending for every verb. |
| 16 | Plans & opinions / 計劃與意見 | Make a plan, give a reason and respond to another opinion. | Use going to for a plan and because for a reason. Respond politely with I agree or I see your point, but ... | Use 打算 + action for a plan, 因为…所以… for cause and result, and 我觉得 to introduce an opinion. |

The new units contain 3,240 authored exercise instances across the two languages and three age bands. The complete courses retain the original food-unit exercises as well.

Progression: children follow the course in order; adults can choose any unit. Review retains comprehension and sentence-building tasks while personalising spoken recall.

Level: broad everyday foundation with longer teen/adult extensions. No CEFR certificate, HSK level completion or advanced mastery is implied.

# Communication lessons — teaching guide

This pack adds 27 lessons in each of English, Putonghua, Japanese, Korean, French and Spanish: 162 lesson identities, 486 age-band versions and 3,186 exercise instances. Repeated practice across lessons and age bands is intentional; these are not 3,186 unique language points.

Open the [lesson index](COMMUNICATION-MAP.md) for the six readable books. App-ready data is in courses/*-communication.json, editable material is in authoring/communication/, and translations are in i18n/communication.json. Rebuild with npm run lessons:communication from the project root.

This is original beginner material with longer polite extensions for teens/adults, not an accredited course or a complete advanced programme. Independent language-teacher review remains pending.

## Outcomes

| Topic | Practice | Practical text |
|---|---|---|
| Getting Around | Destinations, directions, tickets, transport, departure times and return journeys. | Departure notice |
| People & Small Talk | Introductions, home, interests, follow-up questions, invitations and goodbyes. | Personal profile |
| Work & Professional Communication | Role introductions, meetings, messages, repetition, telephone requests and deadlines. | Meeting email |

Each unit has nine short lessons: vocabulary; three situation lessons; listening/clear speech; reading; guided conversation; practical reading/writing; review.

Little learners use short replies and adult-supported drawing or dictation. Juniors use complete everyday sentences. Teens/adults use longer polite exchanges. For children, Work means a school project or team activity. Use fictional names and contact details.

## Language notes

- **English:** Practise Where, What time and When; to the station, by bus and at ten; I would like and Could you. In small talk, add a detail and ask a question back. Distinguish an action promised today from a deadline on Friday.
- **Putonghua:** Model 去/到 for destinations, 从 for a starting point, 往 and 左转/右转 for directions, 张 for tickets and 十点 for clock time. Teach 请/请问, 叫 for a name, 喜欢 for interests and 你呢 for a return question. 最晚星期五 makes a deadline explicit. Both character scripts and numbered citation-tone pinyin are supplied.
- **Japanese:** Teach に/へ, で and the ticket counter 枚 through the examples. Practise polite requests as complete chunks before introducing new conjugations. The particles は, を and へ have their spoken readings encoded. Long vowels and small っ require distinct timing. さん belongs with another person's name, not a self-introduction. までに marks a deadline.
- **Korean:** Practise polite 요 endings, 에/에서, -(으)로, 한 장 and 열 시. The written and pronounced forms are supplied separately: for example 학교 and 학꾜. -(으)ㄹ 수 있어요 expresses ability; -까지 marks a deadline. 안녕히 가세요 is used when the other person leaves.
- **French:** Contrast child-to-child tu with polite vous. Model à la gare, à l'école, au parc and à dix heures. Je voudrais is a polite request; je vais + infinitive describes a plan. The sample uses masculine ravi; teach ravie where appropriate. Dictionary additions use citation forms, not every phrase-level liaison or regional pronunciation.
- **Spanish:** Contrast polite usted questions with child-to-child tú forms. Model a la estación, en autobús and a las diez. Practise quisiera and me gusta / me gustan. The app's reference voice uses es-ES; billete and quedar reflect that variety. Accept appropriate regional alternatives in teacher-led practice.

## Application and feedback

Draw a route and write a departure notice; create a fictional profile and invitation; write a meeting message with greeting, purpose, time, requested action and closing. Change one detail first, then invent a new example. Role-play a follow-up telephone call.

For original writing, a teacher or partner can award 0–2 points each for completing the task, including the important details, understandable meaning and appropriate tone. This eight-point classroom rubric is not a proficiency certificate.

The app checks the supplied reading/listening answers and model sentence order and uses its existing speaking-feedback system. Guided conversations provide model replies; they do not grade every possible open-ended response. Human feedback is needed for free writing and alternative role-play answers. Children may dictate to an adult.

Answer keys are visible in the books for teachers. Hide them during practice. Listening passages are scripts for the teacher or app voice; no publisher recordings are included.

## Editorial status and checks

These lessons are original project material, separate from the downloaded institutional books in library/. English glosses sometimes express the communicative function rather than a word-for-word translation.

Useful institutional references for continued editorial review include the [Japan Foundation's daily-life Can-do list](https://www.jpf.go.jp/e/urawa/e_rsorcs/dl/202310_seikatsu_cando_list_all_en.pdf) and the [National Institute of Korean Language's pronunciation rules](https://www.korean.go.kr/front/page/pageView.do?page_id=P000101). These references do not endorse or certify this pack.

Automated checks cover structure, references, script reconstruction, reading evidence, language routing, French pronunciation coverage, translations and review behaviour. Browser checks exercise all six topic maps, reading answers and sentence building, plus mobile layout and the work email.

These checks do not replace independent review of naturalness, register, pronunciation variants and age suitability. The pack is connected to the local app; publication is a separate step.

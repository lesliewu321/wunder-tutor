Every line of the app’s own wording, English beside the Chinese draft. Read down a section. Where the Chinese is wrong, stiff, or not how Hong Kong families would say it, press Change, type what it should say, and save. Lines you leave alone count as fine. Claude reads your changes from this page and puts them into the app.

  • Only touch what’s wrongThere is nothing to tick per line. Mark a whole section as checked when you reach its end.
  • Keep the code bits{name} is filled in by the app and ** wraps bold words. Move them where the sentence needs them, but keep them.
  • No long dashThe headline font draws —— as 一一. Use ， or ： instead; a long sound is 「嗶～～」.
